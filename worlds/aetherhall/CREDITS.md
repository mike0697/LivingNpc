# Aetherhall – credits

Aetherhall is the example world of LivingNPC: a small school of magic with two characters, a few places and some
messages, to show how a world is made. Its texts and data are released under the same license as the rest of the
repository (MPL-2.0).

## Pictures

The backgrounds (`base/nodes/*/bg.png`), the characters (`base/images/people/`), the secret passage
(`base/images/places/`) and the buttons (`base/images/buttons/`) were generated with AI image tools (ChatGPT) for this
project.
